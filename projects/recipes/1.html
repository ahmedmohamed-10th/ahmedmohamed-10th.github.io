My Mom's Koshary

About Our Koshary

Koshary is a popular Egyptian dish. My mom makes it for our family, and it is one of my favorite meals.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Lentils
  • Macaroni
  • Tomato sauce
  • Crispy onions
  • Hot sauce

How to Make Koshary

Ingredient Amount Instructions
Rice 2 cups Cook until tender.
Lentils 1 cup Boil until soft.
Macaroni 2 cups Boil until cooked.
Tomato sauce 2 cups Heat and season.
Crispy onions 1 cup Prepare until crispy.
Hot sauce To taste Add as desired.

Prep Time and Servings

Cooking Time: About 2 hours.

Servings: A large batch for a family of 4 that lasts about 3 days, with extra to share with other people.
