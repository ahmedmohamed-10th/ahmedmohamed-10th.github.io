My Mom's Ful

About Our Ful

Ful is a popular Egyptian dish made with fava beans. My mom makes it for our family, and it connects us to our Egyptian culture.

Ingredients

  • Fava beans
  • Olive oil
  • Lemon juice
  • Garlic
  • Salt
  • Cumin

How to Make Ful

Ingredient Amount Instructions
Fava beans 2 cans Heat the beans in a pot.
Olive oil 2 tablespoons Mix into the beans.
Lemon juice 2 tablespoons Add to the beans.
Garlic 2 cloves Crush and mix in.
Salt To taste Add a little at a time.
Cumin 1 teaspoon Stir into the beans.

Prep Time and Servings

Cooking Time: About 20 minutes.

Servings: About 4 people.

by our family.
